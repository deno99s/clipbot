Privacy Policy – ClipBot

ClipBot is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload their own video clips to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

  • ClipBot only requests the permissions needed to upload videos (TikTok: user.info.basic, video.upload / video.publish; YouTube: youtube.upload).
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared with third parties.
  • ClipBot does not collect, sell or analyse data of other users.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings.
